# Code of Conduct

Lenguaraz exists so that people who do not share a language can follow the same talk. The
same spirit applies here: anyone who reports a bug, opens a pull request, asks a question or
runs the software at an event should be able to take part without being made unwelcome.

## Expected

- Be respectful and assume good faith; disagree with ideas, not with people.
- Explain, cite and measure instead of asserting; treat review as part of the work.
- Use clear language; welcome contributors whose first language is not English or Spanish.
- Credit prior work honestly; respect licenses and other people's media.

## Not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, discriminatory remarks or sexualized language in any project space.
- Publishing private information or recordings of people without their consent.
- Sustained disruption of discussions, reviews or events where the project is presented.

## Scope

The repository (issues, pull requests, discussions), any chat or mailing channel the project
uses, and representing the project in public, such as a conference deployment or a talk.

## Reporting

Report incidents privately to the maintainer named in `NOTICE`, through the same private
channel described in `SECURITY.md` (GitHub private reporting or email). Reports are read by
the maintainer only, acknowledged within 72 hours and handled with the reporter's privacy in
mind. Outcomes range from a private conversation to removal from project spaces.

This text is original and short on purpose; it is inspired by the
[Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/), the fuller reference we
recommend for standards and enforcement.
